About

About Northmoor Audio.


Northmoor Audio was created to provide straightforward and dependable live sound services for musicians, events, and local productions.


The focus is on making the technical side of an event easier so performers and organizers can concentrate on the experience itself.


Whether it’s mixing a live band, setting up a PA system, or helping troubleshoot an event setup, Northmoor Audio is built around clean sound, attention to detail, and reliable service.
